### A Little Studio for Big Ideas

Corkit is an interactive digital corkboard website that allows users to create personalized vision boards and personalized boards in a fun and creative way.

Inspired by the traditional physical corkboards and vision boards, this project brings the experience into a digital environment where users can collect inspiration, organize their favorite visuals, and express their creativity through a customizable canvas.

Whether you're collecting inspiration, planning your goals, creating a personalized board, or simply experimenting with different aesthetics, Corkit gives you the freedom to make it your own.

## ⤷ ゛About the Project ˎˊ˗

Corkit is designed to recreate the feeling of arranging a physical corkboard, but with the flexibility of a digital workspace.

Users can create their own visual compositions by adding different elements to a board, moving them around, adjusting their sizes, and arranging everything according to their personal style.

The goal is to provide a simple and enjoyable creative experience without limiting users to predefined layouts or templates.

## ᯓ➤ Project Motivation

I've always enjoyed creating physical corkboards. Collecting pictures, choosing colors, adding little decorative details, and arranging everything together is something I genuinely enjoy.

I wanted to bring that experience into a digital space where people can experiment freely, create their own visual stories, and keep all their inspiration in one place.


This project also gave me the opportunity to combine my interest in visual design with my passion for web development and build something inspired by a personal interest.

## ˗ˏˋ  Features  ๋࣭ ⭑

### ⋆ Creative Elements
Add different elements to your board and personalize your design:

- **Photos:** Add pictures and visual inspiration.
- **Stickers:** Decorate your board with creative stickers.
- **Washi Tapes:** Add decorative tape elements.
- **Pins:** Give your board a traditional corkboard look.
- **Text:** Add notes, quotes, titles, and personal messages.

### ⋆ Interactive Editing
Customize your board with flexible editing controls:

- Press on elements and position them freely.
- Resize elements to fit your layout.
- Rotate elements to create different visual arrangements.
- Combine different elements to create unique compositions.

### ⋆ Export & Community Sharing
- Download your finished boards as high-quality PNG images.
- Share your creative boards with the Corkit community.
- Explore and discover boards created by other users.
- Get inspired by other people's ideas and creative styles.
- Showcase your designs and share your visual inspiration with others.

### ⋆ Personalization
- Create your own visual arrangements.
- Experiment with different combinations of images and decorative elements.
- Design boards that reflect your personal style and inspiration.

## .ᐟ How to Use

1. Open the Corkit website.
2. Start creating your board.
3. Add photos, stickers, washi tapes, pins, and text.
4. Drag elements around to arrange your composition.
5. Resize and rotate elements until you are happy with the result.
6. Download your finished board as a PNG image or share it with our community!

There are no fixed creative rules, design your board however you like ☺︎

Corkit was built using the following technologies:

---

## 🗁 Backend & Data Management

Corkit uses Supabase to manage its backend functionality and community features.

- PostgreSQL Database: Manages application data and community submissions.
- Supabase Storage: Stores uploaded images and community content.
- Approval Workflow: Allows community submissions to be reviewed and approved before being displayed publicly.
This allows users to save and restore their work without requiring a backend database or user account.

## ➜] Board Export

Users can download their completed vision boards as PNG images directly to their devices, allowing them to keep and share their creative designs outside the platform.

note: Board exports are downloaded locally and are not automatically saved to the Supabase database.
